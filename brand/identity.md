# Galio visual identity options

Status: pre-launch exploration · 2026-08-10

> **Current Gallium exploration:** `concepts/05-full-brand-variations/` contains four separate identity versions—Cool Living Metal, Warm Living Metal, Spectral Standard, and Civic Alloy. The older Liquid Standard, Phase 29.76, Float, and Skin routes remain as process history. Each current version includes Galio Open, the free pentesting program for selected nonprofits, as a first-class brand expression.

> **Focused directions:** Cool Living Metal and Warm Living Metal are independent v0.4 identities using Liquid Standard’s original Sora, Manrope, and IBM Plex Mono typography. Biomorphic geometry structures UI components, evidence paths, dividers, reports, and Galio Open in both, but no palette, artwork, or application may combine the two versions. Open `concepts/05-full-brand-variations/living-metal/guide.html` for Cool or `concepts/05-full-brand-variations/living-metal/guide-warm.html` for Warm.

These are three alternative identity systems. Choose one territory, refine its mark, and test it with buyers before production rollout.

## Shared identity strategy

Galio should look like an expert security partner that challenges the annual, point-in-time pentest without manufacturing anxiety. The system must express two truths together: relentless testing while work is underway and calm, legible confidence after findings are resolved. It should feel credible to enterprise security leaders, contemporary to SaaS teams, and technically native to software factories. AI is shown as leverage inside a human-accountable process—not as a substitute for expert judgment.

## Option 1 — Galio: Quiet Defiance

### 01 — Identity strategy statement

Quiet Defiance positions Galio as the category challenger with the restraint of a premium technical product. A broken circular `G` becomes a continuous-testing loop whose deliberate opening signals that assumptions are always testable; the square terminal is the human decision point. High-contrast acid and midnight create energy, while generous space and disciplined typography keep the result calm. This is the most memorable and extensible option.

### 02 — Logo direction

- **Primary direction**: Custom symbol plus lowercase geometric wordmark.
- **Character**: Restless in method, controlled in delivery.
- **Style**: Constructed, monoline, open geometry; a circular `G` interrupted by a human-validation node.
- **Avoid**: Shields, locks, crosshairs, bug icons, circuit-board decoration, glowing cyberpunk effects.
- **References**:
  - [Linear](https://linear.app/brand) — borrow disciplined monochrome restraint and generous clear space, not its artwork.
  - [Vercel](https://vercel.com/geist/brands) — borrow the economy of a simple geometric symbol and strong wordmark relationship.
  - [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/) — borrow system-level rigor and the ability to scale from editorial to technical UI.

### 03 — Color palette

- **Proof Black** `#0B1020` — primary ground and authority.
- **Live Acid** `#C7FF4A` — action, active testing, and decisive CTAs.
- **Clear White** `#F5F7F2` — calm working surface.
- **Verification Teal** `#4FD1C5` — resolved states and human validation.
- **Trace Gray** `#758096` — metadata only.
- **Type**: High-contrast complementary accent system. Acid is never used for body text on white.

### 04 — Typography

- **Primary**: Space Grotesk, 400–700. Geometric but not sterile; use for display and body.
- **Secondary**: IBM Plex Mono, 400–600. Use for evidence IDs, metrics, labels, and code—not paragraphs.
- **Hierarchy**: H1 64–88/0.92, H2 36–48/1.0, H3 22–28/1.15, body 16–20/1.55, label 12–13 uppercase with 0.10em tracking.
- **Fallback**: `Arial, sans-serif` and `ui-monospace, monospace`.

### 05 — Imagery & photography

Use close, documentary images of real collaboration, testing artifacts, and quiet post-remediation moments. Crop decisively and leave negative space. Prefer neutral color with a single acid annotation. Avoid hoodies, server-room blue light, staged handshakes, and anonymous scrolling code.

### 06 — Iconography & illustration

Use 1.75px open-line icons built on a 24px grid, with square terminals and one intentional interruption. Diagrams should resemble evidence maps: nodes, paths, states, and annotations—never decorative circuitry.

### 07 — Design principles

- **Expose the seam**: Show where automated breadth hands off to human judgment. The identity is strongest when the process is visible.
- **Calm is earned**: Use space, hierarchy, and plain findings to create reassurance after rigorous work.
- **One live signal**: Use acid only for the single most important action or active state.
- **Evidence over theater**: Every graphic should clarify risk, progress, or resolution.

### 08 — Brand expressions

- **Website**: Dark, editorial hero; acid CTA; proof metrics directly below the promise.
- **Client portal**: Mostly light surfaces; acid for active testing and teal for verified remediation.
- **Reports**: White, typographic, with black findings and precise severity tags.
- **Social**: One claim, one evidence fragment, one interrupted-ring motif.
- **Pitch decks**: Large declarative headlines, short proof bars, no gradient backgrounds.

## Option 2 — Galio Security: Continuous Signal

### 01 — Identity strategy statement

Continuous Signal makes the service model instantly visible. Two opposing brackets represent AI-assisted coverage and human expertise, while a central pulse represents the verified signal that matters. Cobalt carries technical confidence; coral adds an unmistakably human, nonconformist note. This option is the most energetic and commercially explicit.

### 02 — Logo direction

- **Primary direction**: Paired-bracket symbol plus two-line wordmark.
- **Character**: Alert, collaborative, always in motion.
- **Style**: Modular and asymmetrical; the mark should work as a portal status icon.
- **Avoid**: Radar screens, heartbeat clichés, literal robots, split “human vs AI” imagery.
- **References**:
  - [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-kit/) — borrow warmth and visibility in a technical category.
  - [GitLab](https://design.gitlab.com/brand-logo/core-logo) — borrow bold color ownership and an identifiable standalone mark.
  - [Atlassian](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos) — borrow a coherent system that works across product and marketing contexts.

### 03 — Color palette

- **System Ink** `#111827` — text and dark backgrounds.
- **Continuous Blue** `#3157F6` — primary brand and links.
- **Human Coral** `#FF6B5F` — human review, challenges, and campaign accent.
- **Warm Field** `#F7F3EA` — primary editorial surface.
- **Resolved Mint** `#B9F5D8` — verified outcomes.
- **Type**: Split-complementary signal palette. Never place coral text on warm field at body sizes.

### 04 — Typography

- **Primary**: Manrope, 400–800. Clear, approachable, and strong in SaaS interfaces.
- **Secondary**: IBM Plex Mono, 400–600. For technical evidence and continuous-status data.
- **Hierarchy**: H1 60–80/0.95, H2 36–44/1.05, H3 22–26/1.2, body 16–19/1.55, label 12–13 uppercase.
- **Fallback**: `Arial, sans-serif` and `ui-monospace, monospace`.

### 05 — Imagery & photography

Use real teams in working sessions, paired with structured overlays that connect action to finding to retest. Natural skin tones and warm environments should counter the cold conventions of cybersecurity. Avoid generic offices, fake SOC walls, and red danger treatments.

### 06 — Iconography & illustration

Use 2px rounded-line icons on a 24px grid. Brackets, connectors, and small pulses create a repeatable visual grammar. Icons are functional first and may use one coral node to denote human action.

### 07 — Design principles

- **Always in sequence**: Show movement from discover → verify → resolve → retest.
- **Human in the loop**: Coral marks accountable expert decisions, never decorative emphasis.
- **Warm precision**: Pair strict grids with warm surfaces and plain language.
- **Show the status**: Make progress and recency visible at every touchpoint.

### 08 — Brand expressions

- **Website**: Warm field, cobalt navigation, animated bracket/pulse motif.
- **Client portal**: Timeline-led UI with explicit “human verified” states.
- **Reports**: Modular cards and remediation sequences.
- **Social**: Contrarian category claims on cobalt with coral annotations.
- **Pitch decks**: Split-screen narrative: point-in-time problem versus continuous model.

## Option 3 — Galio CyberSecurity: Assured Horizon

### 01 — Identity strategy statement

Assured Horizon prioritizes enterprise reassurance without becoming conventional. A stepped horizon suggests continuous observation and the calmer operating state achieved after remediation; the negative-space `G` keeps the symbol proprietary. Deep pine and mineral mint feel established and composed, while a small amber signal retains edge. This is the safest option for conservative procurement environments, though the long name is less distinctive.

### 02 — Logo direction

- **Primary direction**: Horizon symbol plus editorial wordmark and compact descriptor.
- **Character**: Assured, measured, durable.
- **Style**: Architectural curves, restrained contrast, minimal movement.
- **Avoid**: Institutional crests, bank-like seals, glossy gradients, landscape illustration, vague “peace of mind” imagery.
- **References**:
  - [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/) — borrow enterprise authority and editorial discipline.
  - [Linear](https://linear.app/brand) — borrow confidence through restraint and consistent monochrome applications.
  - [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-kit/) — borrow the ability to communicate serious infrastructure with a recognizable, non-shield symbol.

### 03 — Color palette

- **Deep Pine** `#073B3A` — primary brand, text, and trust.
- **Mineral Mint** `#D8F3E7` — calm surface and resolved states.
- **Signal Amber** `#F2B84B` — sparse action and attention accent.
- **Paper** `#F8FAF7` — editorial background.
- **Basalt** `#27312F` — secondary text.
- **Type**: Analogous green foundation with a controlled warm accent.

### 04 — Typography

- **Primary display**: Newsreader, 500–700. Editorial confidence and a human counterpoint to technical sameness.
- **Secondary**: Manrope, 400–700. Clear interface and long-form report typography.
- **Utility**: IBM Plex Mono for evidence IDs only.
- **Hierarchy**: H1 58–76/0.98, H2 36–46/1.05, H3 22–26/1.2, body 16–19/1.6, label 12 uppercase.
- **Fallback**: `Georgia, serif`; `Arial, sans-serif`; `ui-monospace, monospace`.

### 05 — Imagery & photography

Use natural, daylight documentary photography of teams after decisions: reviewing a resolved path, planning a release, or working with nonprofit partners. Pair with quiet abstract horizons derived from report data. Avoid sunsets, clasped hands, boardroom clichés, and over-retouched executive portraits.

### 06 — Iconography & illustration

Use 1.5px architectural line icons, rounded outer corners, and flat terminals. Illustrations are sparse data landscapes made from horizontal evidence bands and single amber markers.

### 07 — Design principles

- **Clarity lowers pressure**: Lead with the conclusion, then expose the evidence.
- **Durable, not static**: Use stable forms with subtle progression to imply continuous work.
- **Human scale**: Editorial type and documentary imagery keep expertise visible.
- **Amber is a decision**: The warm accent always indicates action or attention.

### 08 — Brand expressions

- **Website**: Light editorial layout with pine navigation and amber CTA.
- **Client portal**: Calm status summaries with evidence available on demand.
- **Reports**: Executive-ready typography and clear remediation ownership.
- **Social**: Insight-led excerpts and nonprofit impact stories.
- **Pitch decks**: Large editorial statements followed by restrained proof tables.

## Option 4 — Galio: Liquid Standard

> Refinement note: the original `Ga / 31` tile was judged too generic. It has been superseded for decision-making by the three proprietary territories in `concepts/04-gallium-variations/variations.md`: **Phase 29.76**, **Float**, and **Skin**. The detailed section below remains as exploration history.

### 01 — Identity strategy statement

Liquid Standard turns the physical behavior of elemental gallium into a proprietary brand idea: solid rigor with fluid coverage. Gallium’s silvery appearance and unusually low melting point create a bright, tactile alternative to dark cybersecurity conventions. The system remains white, exact, and enterprise-ready while its metallic forms adapt around content. The metaphor supports continuous testing without making scientific properties stand in for security proof.

### 02 — Logo direction

- **Primary direction**: Elemental `Ga / 31` symbol plus Galio wordmark and launch descriptor.
- **Character**: Precise, adaptive, tactile, and unexpectedly bright.
- **Style**: A strict tile broken by one liquid-metal terminal; flat monochrome fallback for production constraints.
- **Avoid**: Chrome wordmarks, mercury beads, liquid-metal robots, chemistry-class decoration, and claims that Galio is a material-science company.
- **References**:
  - [Royal Society of Chemistry’s gallium profile](https://periodic-table.rsc.org/element/31/Gallium) — use the authentic element symbol, atomic number, and silvery material territory.
  - [NIST gallium melting-point standard](https://www.nist.gov/publications/standard-reference-material-1751-gallium-melting-point-standard) — borrow the idea of exact standards and controlled state change.
  - [IBM Design Language](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/) — borrow scientific authority, modular structure, and disciplined information design.

### 03 — Color palette

- **Laboratory White** `#FFFFFF` — dominant field, not empty background.
- **Gallium Silver** `#BBC4CC` base — material accents and photographic forms.
- **Graphite** `#20262D` — primary text and monochrome mark.
- **Ion Blue** `#2D64F0` — links, active testing, and decisions.
- **Vapor Blue** `#EDF3FF` — selected surfaces and diagrams.
- **Type**: White-led neutral system with one high-clarity blue accent. Metallic gradients are treated as material, never text color.

### 04 — Typography

- **Primary display**: Sora, 500–700. Geometric, engineered, and open.
- **Secondary**: Manrope, 400–700. Long-form report and interface text.
- **Utility**: IBM Plex Mono, 400–600 for evidence IDs, atomic references, and states.
- **Hierarchy**: H1 60–82/0.96, H2 36–46/1.05, body 16–19/1.6, label 12 uppercase.
- **Fallback**: `Arial, sans-serif` and `ui-monospace, monospace`.

### 05 — Imagery & photography

Use photorealistic macro studies of actual gallium transitioning from crystalline solid to cohesive liquid on bright laboratory-white surfaces. Preserve controlled silver reflections, real texture, and generous negative space. Avoid molten heat, chrome CGI, scattered mercury-like beads, and unrelated laboratory equipment.

### 06 — Iconography & illustration

Use 1.5px graphite line icons on a 24px grid with a single softened or fluid terminal. Data diagrams may pair rigid grids with one adaptive silver boundary. Ion Blue always identifies action or current state.

### 07 — Design principles

- **Fluid within constraints**: The grid remains exact while one material form adapts around content.
- **Reflect what is true**: Metallic surfaces can reveal or frame evidence; they never add decorative noise.
- **White is active**: Space creates precision, calm, and material contrast.
- **Element, not gimmick**: `Ga / 31` is a signature used sparingly, not a periodic-table theme applied everywhere.

### 08 — Brand expressions

- **Website**: White hero, real gallium material study, Graphite headline, and one Ion Blue action.
- **Client portal**: White/light-gray surfaces with thin metallic separators and explicit current-state labels.
- **Reports**: Monochrome-first production with a small `Ga / 31` verification stamp.
- **Social**: Macro material crops paired with short claims about continuous state change.
- **Pitch decks**: Rigid evidence grids interrupted by a single fluid silver form.
