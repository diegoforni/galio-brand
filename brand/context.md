# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Galio (working master brand; Galio Security and Galio CyberSecurity remain naming candidates)
- **Category**: Offensive cybersecurity / continuous penetration testing
- **Description**: A pentesting agency combining AI-assisted coverage with human expertise, offering continuous security testing and free engagements for selected nonprofit organizations.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch
- **Website**: N/A

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Security and technology decision-makers at SaaS companies, enterprises, and software factories in Argentina and English-speaking markets.
- **Key Problem**: Point-in-time pentests create a temporary snapshot, while software and threats keep changing. Buyers need rigorous, understandable findings and continued assurance without sacrificing expert human judgment.
- **Their Language**: Evidence, exploitable risk, remediation, continuous coverage, attack surface, release confidence, clear findings, fast retesting, human validation, business impact.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Galio combines AI-assisted breadth and speed with accountable human-led testing, favors an ongoing testing relationship over a once-a-year engagement, and reserves selected pro-bono engagements for nonprofits.
- **Competitors**: No specific competitors supplied; the practical alternative set is traditional consultancies, automated scanners, and pentest-as-a-service platforms.
- **Market Position**: Mid-market to premium; specialist, high-touch, and outcome-focused rather than commodity testing.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Nonconformist, rigorous, calm, candid, vigilant
- **Tone**: Serious without fearmongering; direct and confident with a controlled edge.
- **Voice Admires**: None specified.

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Human accountability, continuous improvement, useful truth, calm through evidence, security as public good
- **Mission**: Make rigorous offensive security more continuous, more useful, and more accessible by combining AI-assisted coverage with expert human judgment—and extending that protection to selected nonprofits at no cost.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Assumption for launch planning — establish credibility and convert qualified design partners or pilot clients in the first 12 months.
- **Key Metrics**: Assumption for launch planning — qualified conversations, pilot engagements, recurring pentest clients, remediation/retest completion, and nonprofit engagements delivered.

## Working Constraints
- **Markets and languages**: Argentina plus English-speaking markets; the identity should support Spanish and English.
- **Primary touchpoints**: Website, security reports, client portal/product UI, proposals, presentations, social posts, and event materials.
- **Avoid**: Generic shields, padlocks, hooded-hacker imagery, neon-on-black “cyberpunk,” alarmist copy, and claims that imply AI replaces expert testers.
