# Galio brand essentials — shared foundation

This document records the strategic rules shared by all three visual options. The visual details are alternatives; do not mix palettes, marks, or typography between concepts.

## Brand foundation

- **Mission**: Make rigorous offensive security more continuous, useful, and accessible by combining AI-assisted coverage with expert human judgment—and extending protection to selected nonprofits at no cost.
- **Vision**: A software industry where assurance keeps pace with every release, not an annual compliance date.
- **Values**: Human accountability; continuous improvement; useful truth; calm through evidence; security as a public good.
- **Personality**: Nonconformist, rigorous, calm, candid, vigilant.
- **Positioning**: For SaaS companies, enterprises, and software factories underserved by point-in-time testing, Galio is the continuous offensive-security partner that combines AI-assisted breadth with accountable human expertise, producing evidence teams can act on and confidence they can sustain.

## Recommended naming architecture

- **Master brand**: `Galio`
- **Launch descriptor**: `Continuous offensive security`
- **SEO / explanatory form**: `Galio Security`
- **Avoid as primary name**: `Galio CyberSecurity`; it is clear but long, generic, and visually cumbersome. Use “cybersecurity” as category language when context requires it.
- **Capitalization**: Galio, never GALIO in running text. Use sentence case in headlines.

## Verbal identity

- **Voice essence**: Clear enough to lower anxiety, candid enough to earn trust, and sharp enough to challenge stale security practice.
- **Direct, not dramatic**: “This path exposes customer records” rather than “A devastating breach is imminent.”
- **Expert, not opaque**: Explain the exploit and business effect; never hide behind jargon.
- **Confident, not absolute**: Say what was tested, when, and with what confidence. Never promise perfect security.
- **Nonconformist, not reckless**: Challenge point-in-time testing with evidence, not swagger.
- **Human, not anthropomorphic AI**: AI assists coverage and prioritization; named experts validate and decide.

### Writing rules

- Lead with the conclusion; follow with evidence and next action.
- Prefer 8–18 word sentences in marketing copy; reports may be longer when precision requires it.
- Use sentence case, active voice, contractions in conversational channels, and numerals for metrics.
- Write `pentest`, `retest`, `AI-assisted`, `human-verified`, and `continuous testing` consistently.
- Spanish should be neutral Rioplatense professional Spanish; avoid translating technical terms buyers already use in English.

### We say

Continuous testing · human-verified · exploitable risk · clear evidence · remediation path · retest included · current assurance · expert-led · attack surface · release confidence · useful findings · selected nonprofit partners.

### We do not say

Unhackable · 100% secure · AI pentester · replace your security team · military-grade · next-gen · cutting-edge · single pane of glass · fear the breach · hackers never sleep · guaranteed compliance · charity case.

## Messaging

- **Core message**: Your software changes continuously. Your pentest should too.
- **Value proposition**: Galio combines AI-assisted coverage with human-led offensive testing to find exploitable risk faster, explain it clearly, and keep validating as software changes. Clients get a continuous security relationship—not a report that starts aging the day it arrives.
- **Recommended tagline**: `Test continuously. Ship calmly.`
- **Supporting message 1**: AI expands coverage; experts own the conclusion.
- **Supporting message 2**: Findings are written for remediation, not theater.
- **Supporting message 3**: Retesting turns fixes into verified confidence.
- **Supporting message 4**: Selected nonprofits receive the same standard of work at no cost.

## Universal logo rules

- Keep clear space equal to the symbol’s internal node or one lowercase `o`, whichever is larger.
- Minimum digital size: 24px for the symbol; 112px wide for the full lockup.
- Minimum print size: 7mm for the symbol; 30mm wide for the lockup.
- Use supplied artwork only. Do not stretch, outline, shadow, rotate, redraw, or combine options.
- Use one-color marks on photography, with sufficient contrast and a quiet background area.

## Accessibility and production

- Body text and controls must meet WCAG AA contrast: 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text/UI graphics.
- Never communicate severity by color alone; pair color with a label and icon.
- All three systems use typefaces available through Google Fonts. Package font files before production launch to remove third-party runtime dependencies.
- SVG assets are editable concept artwork. Complete optical refinement, trademark screening, favicon testing, and small-size testing after one route is selected.

## Brand in use

- **Website**: Lead with the continuous-testing problem, show the AI-plus-human method, then prove it with process and sample outputs.
- **Reports**: Use a stable information hierarchy: conclusion → business impact → evidence → remediation → retest status.
- **Portal**: Show testing recency, human validation, ownership, and remediation state at a glance.
- **Social**: Teach and challenge. One useful claim per post; avoid breach-news fearjacking.
- **Nonprofit program**: Frame it as equal-standard access and partnership. Never use pity imagery or a visually “discounted” sub-brand.

## Decision recommendation

Test **Galio / Quiet Defiance** against **Galio / Liquid Standard** with early buyers. Quiet Defiance has the strongest category-challenger energy; Liquid Standard has the most ownable origin story and a brighter, less conventional cybersecurity look. Use the same launch descriptor—`Continuous offensive security`—so the identity test does not become a category-clarity test.
